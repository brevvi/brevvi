## Hi, I'm Brevvi

Systems architect and independent researcher of cyberculture. I work on **signed information**: websites, databases and documents for small and medium companies in which every data point carries its source, and information gaps are pointed out, not hidden. Services are delivered through [Silverbullet Research](https://silverbullet.live), the company I founded, with the instruments it builds. My research and published work live at [brevvi.com](https://brevvi.com).

This space shares the projects I build in the open: course apps, study labs and experiments.
